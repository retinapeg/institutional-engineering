# Contributing

Research only; product work belongs in institutional-workbench.
Pre-register tasks, graders, extraction rules, budgets and selection before inference.
Keep failures, denominators and immutable observations; disclose protocol deviations.
Never rewrite Pilot 0. Synthetic data only; no secrets/account identifiers; mock CI.
No live calls without authorization, account changes or purchases.
Keep role, model and protocol effects separate. Claims must follow evidence.
