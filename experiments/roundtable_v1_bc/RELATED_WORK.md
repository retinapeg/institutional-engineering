## Claim limits

These papers already examine role/model distinctions, multi-agent coding, debate and structured information flow. Roundtable v1 does not claim to be the first study of any of those ideas. Its narrow proposed contribution is the B/C contrast under an otherwise fixed institution and executable scoring policy.

The prior studies differ in tasks, models, interventions and compute accounting. Their reported gains or nulls do not predict this study's sign, validate the current task pack, or resolve the present grader-integrity blocker. A negative result could be consistent with anchoring or overspecialization but would not establish a mechanism.
